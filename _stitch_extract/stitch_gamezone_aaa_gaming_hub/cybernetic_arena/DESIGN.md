## Brand & Style

This design system defines an esports and AAA gaming ecosystem engineered for competitive tension, high-stakes tournament tracking, and visceral sensory engagement. The design identity merges dark cyberpunk ergonomics with elite arena broadcast styling. 

Targeted at hardcore gamers, esports athletes, tournament organizers, and broadcast spectators, the interface prioritizes hyper-legible telemetry, split-second readability, and tactical authority. 

### Visual Identity & Aesthetic Directives
- **Atmospheric Obsidian Void:** The backdrop relies on deep, light-absorbing obsidian layers rather than flat black, creating a stadium-at-midnight canvas where glowing metrics cut through with laser sharpness.
- **Controlled Neon Emission:** High-voltage cyan and violet are reserved strictly for interactive state vectors, live broadcast indicators, and telemetry accents. Glow is tight, directional, and physical—never muddy or overblown.
- **Angular HUD Precision:** Interfaces evoke high-performance heads-up displays (HUDs), utilizing chamfered edges, hairline grid lines, technical data readouts, and illuminated edge rimming.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive dark-mode paradigm anchored by midnight obsidian and pierced by calibrated neon channels.

### Structural Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#0A0A12`):** Primary canvas substrate; absorbs glare and focuses user eye-tracking on high-density data.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0D0E1A`):** Low-tier panels, background cards, and table structures.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#141526`):** Active interactive surfaces, popovers, and elevated modal backdrops.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#1E2038`):** Raised states, active controls, and hover-state overlays.
- **Hairline Border Neutral (`#26294A`):** Standard non-illuminated surface containment lines.

### High-Voltage Accent System
- **Electric Cyber Cyan (`#00F0FF`):** Primary driver for active CTA triggers, focused inputs, confirmed connectivity states, and digital link points.
- **Vivid Neon Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** Secondary driver for user identity, team branding, matchmaking states, and squad progression.
- **Hyper Magenta (`#EC4899`):** High-priority alerts, critical eliminations, killfeed highlights, and maximum damage tiers.
- **Electric Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Tournament championship milestones, prestige XP progression, gold tier rank insignia, and loot assets.

### Functional Semantics
- **Live Broadcast Red (`#EF4444`):** Live stream indicators, in-progress matches, and active server alarms.
- **Target Vector Green (`#10B981`):** Victory confirmations, ping stability, and ready-check completions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a high-contrast dual-family architecture: an aggressive, angular geometric voice for headers, telemetry, and ranks, supported by an ultra-clean, legible grotesque for dense competitive statistics.

### Type Hierarchy Directives
- **Headings & Display:** `Space Grotesk` delivers mechanical precision, wide geometric proportions, and angular cuts that reinforce the arena tournament atmosphere. Headers use tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) for imposing mass.
- **Telemetry & Labels:** All uppercase tags, status markers (`LIVE`, `UPCOMING`), and HUD counters employ `Space Grotesk` with wide letter spacing (`+0.08em` to `+0.1em`) to replicate mil-spec tactical readouts.
- **Body & Dense HUD Data:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides open apertures and neutral legibility, eliminating reading fatigue across leaderboards, player bios, chat channels, and rulebooks.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a 12-column dynamic grid designed for multi-panel esports streaming dashboards, ladder ranks, and match detail breakdowns.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column layout with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates three-pane operational views (navigation dock, primary broadcast/telemetry canvas, and squad/bracket sidebar).
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins. Side panels fold into overlay sheets; match fixtures stack into dynamic card matrices.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Navigation shifts to a docked bottom tactical bar. Leaderboard rows switch from multi-column tables to dense swipeable stat-cards.

### Spatial Rhythm
Dense metric dashboards pair tight internal component spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) with expansive section bounding (`space-xl`), isolating discrete combat intelligence modules without visual collision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional soft drop shadows in favor of tinted backdrops, glassmorphic refraction, and laser-precise edge illumination.

### Depth Stratification
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#0A0A12` non-reflective backdrop. Subtle radial gradients of `#8B5CF6` at 4% opacity ground key sections.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Brackets):** Background `#0D0E1A` with 75% alpha, backed by a `16px` backdrop blur. Enclosed by a 1px border of `#26294A`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules):** Background `#141526` with 85% alpha, `20px` backdrop blur, and a 1px top-highlight border tinted with `#00F0FF` at 20% opacity.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Background `#141526` with 95% alpha, reinforced by an ambient drop shadow: `0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` combined with a perimeter glow: `0 0 25px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.

### Rim-Lighting Directives
Cards, modals, and badge containers feature an asymmetric 1px border gradient. The top and leading edges capture light (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`), fading into the base border `#26294A` along bottom edges to simulate harsh top-down arena stadium lights.

## Shapes

The interface implements a strictly controlled soft-chassis aesthetic (`roundedness: 1`), keeping corner radii between `4px` (`0.25rem`) and `8px` (`0.5rem`).

### Geometric Directives
- **Structural Precision:** Rounded curves never exceed `0.5rem`, preventing soft, consumer-grade aesthetics. Corners feel milled and technical.
- **Chamfered Variants:** Key tactical elements (e.g., "DEPLOY" buttons, tournament bracket seed markers, and active status tags) permit a `45-degree` corner clip (`6px` chamfer) on top-right and bottom-left vertices.
- **Progress Channels:** XP bars, health pools, and countdown indicators run in hard-edged or `2px`-radius rectangular tracks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber (Deploy/Join):** Solid `#00F0FF` fill with `#0A0A12` uppercase bold label. Hover triggers an exterior glow (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`) and a subtle scale shift. Active state drops opacity to 90%.
- **Secondary Arena:** Deep background `#141526` with a 1px border in `#8B5CF6`. Violet neon text with interior glow on hover (`inset 0 0 10px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`).
- **Ghost Tactical:** Transparent background, `#FFFFFF` text at 70% opacity, 1px border of `#26294A`. Hover illuminates the border to `#00F0FF` and text to 100%.

### Badges & Match Status Indicators
- **`LIVE` Tag:** Capsule with background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`, 1px border in `#EF4444`. Includes a flashing circular beacon (`#EF4444`, 1Hz pulse) alongside uppercase `Space Grotesk` text.
- **`UPCOMING` Tag:** Capsule with background `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.1)`, 1px border in `#00F0FF`, and `#00F0FF` typography.
- **`FINISHED` Tag:** Neutral background `rgba(38, 41, 74, 0.4)`, 1px border in `#26294A`, muted silver text.

### Interactive Cards & Tournament Tiles
- **Glassmorphic Base:** 80% opacity `#0D0E1A`, 12px blur, 1px perimeter border transitioning from `#00F0FF` (top-left) to `#26294A` (bottom-right).
- **Hover Micro-State:** Card lifts 2px along the Y-axis; top border brightness increases by 40%; a faint cyan radial sheen follows cursor movement.

### Input Fields
- Dark well background `#0A0A12` with inset border `#26294A` and `0.25rem` corners.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#00F0FF` accompanied by a tight shadow: `0 0 8px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`. Placeholder text sits at 40% white opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (checkbox) and diamond-profile (radio) structures built with 1px `#26294A` borders.
- Active states feature solid `#00F0FF` or `#EC4899` fills with an obsidian central glyph and edge glow.

### XP & Rank Tier Trackers
- Segmented linear tracks with `#0A0A12` backgrounds and hairline dividers.
- Progress fills use neon gradients (`#8B5CF6` transitioning to `#00F0FF` for standard rank; solid `#F59E0B` for Prestige tiers) backed by an active-edge neon pulse.
