Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a squad for a tournament?

Pick a tournament on the home page, then send us the squad name and roster through the contact form with the topic “Tournament registration”. Check-in instructions and your bracket seed arrive by email before the start time.

Why does the site say the contact form is a demo?

This build runs entirely in your browser without a server. The form checks your input and shows a confirmation, but nothing is emailed yet. Connect a form service like Formspree (or your own API) and submissions will start arriving.

How do I report a cheater or a bug?

Use the contact form with the topic “Report a cheater or bug”. Include the match ID, the approximate time and any clip links — reports with match IDs are reviewed by the integrity team within one match cycle.

Is my personal data stored anywhere?

Not in this demo. Nothing you type leaves your device unless a form service is connected. On the live platform, profiles only store your gamer tag, region and match telemetry; you can request deletion at any time by email.
